01 — The Problem

Urban India is overloaded.
And there is nowhere to go.

India's cities are among the most sensory-overwhelming environments on earth. The noise, the crowds, the commutes, the screens. People are burning out in real time — and the only solutions available are expensive, time-consuming, or simply not designed for the Indian context.

01

No private space in public

Malls, airports, and campuses are loud and crowded. There is no place to sit quietly, decompress, or focus for 15 minutes without being observed or disturbed. Privacy is a luxury in Indian public spaces.

02

Wellness is slow and expensive

Spas, retreats, and meditation centres require bookings, travel, and high spend. A working professional or student cannot afford a weekend escape every time stress peaks — they need something fast and affordable.

03

Screen fatigue is invisible

Indians average over 6.5 hours of screen time daily. The result is cognitive overload that standard breaks cannot fix — because those breaks typically involve more screens. What's missing is a true sensory reset.

04

Generic experiences, no cultural resonance

Existing wellness tech — VR headsets, white noise apps, spas — offers generic, Western-influenced content. Nothing is designed around the specific emotional landscapes that resonate with Indian users.

05

Mental health stigma blocks access

In India, seeking mental wellness support still carries stigma. People are far more likely to use a clearly-labelled "relaxation pod" than attend a therapy session — lowering the barrier to entry through reframing.

06

Nature is increasingly inaccessible

Rapid urbanisation means millions of Indians live without regular access to green spaces, water bodies, or natural landscapes. The emotional benefit of nature — proven by research — is becoming a privilege.

02 — The Tech Plan

Virtual Production technology,
repurposed for wellness.

VP Core Tech

The Virtual Production Connection

TranquilPods uses the same foundational technology as Hollywood LED volume stages — high-resolution 360° curved LED walls displaying photorealistic, real-time environments. Where filmmakers use this to shoot actors against virtual backgrounds, TranquilPods uses it to place real people inside immersive, calming Indian landscapes. The technology is identical. The application is completely new.

Phase 1

The 360° LED Environment

The interior walls and ceiling of each pod are lined with high-resolution, curved LED panels displaying photorealistic visuals of Indian locations — pre-rendered in Unreal Engine 5 with real-time lighting that shifts across the 15-minute session (dawn to midday at a Varanasi ghat, for example).

  • 4K+ resolution curved LED panels — floor, 3 walls, ceiling
  • Environments built in Unreal Engine 5
  • Dynamic lighting that shifts naturally over 15 minutes
  • No headset — full open-eye immersion, no motion sickness
Phase 2

Multi-Sensory Layering

The visual environment is synchronised with three additional sensory channels that deepen the sense of actually being there — not just watching a screen. This is what separates a TranquilPod from a TV in a booth.

  • Spatial 3D audio — wave sounds, birdsong, wind, tuned to location
  • Scent diffusion — jasmine, salt air, mountain pine, earth after rain
  • Haptic chair — subtle vibrations for wave swell, wind, distant thunder
  • Mood lighting — ambient glow synced to on-screen colour temperature
Phase 3

AI Personalisation Layer

On first use, the TranquilPods app asks what the person needs — relaxation, focus, energy, or sleep. The system recommends an environment and, over time, learns preferences, time of day patterns, and how long it takes each user to reach a calm state.

  • App-based mood check-in before each session
  • AI-recommended environment based on current need
  • Spotify integration — play personal music inside the environment
  • Session history and "calm score" trend over time
Phase 4

Physical Pod Design

The pod itself is a compact, sound-insulated unit designed for rapid installation in any high-footfall location. Each unit can be placed, powered on, and operational within hours — no construction required.

  • 1.8m × 1.8m × 2.2m — fits in any spare corridor or corner space
  • Sound-insulated shell — external noise reduced by 85%
  • Convertible recliner — sit, relax, nap positions
  • Optional: mini-desk, WiFi, device charging, mini-fridge

07 — How It Runs

The operations
behind the calm.

Every TranquilPod is managed by a dedicated on-site attendant. The experience only works if the pod feels pristine every single time — so the reset between sessions is treated with the same care as the session itself.

Pod Attendant

A human reset
after every session

A trained attendant is stationed at every TranquilPods unit. Between each session — during the mandatory 5-minute turnover window — they enter the pod to wipe down all surfaces, sanitise the recliner and headrest, replace the disposable cushion cover, restock the mini-fridge if applicable, and confirm the pod is ready for the next user before unlocking the door.

Cleanliness is non-negotiable. A single bad experience with hygiene destroys repeat usage — so the attendant checklist is 18 steps and digitally logged after each turnover.

Scent Reset System

No scent bleeds
between guests

The moment a session ends, a dedicated exhaust fan activates automatically — a high-powered ventilation cycle that fully purges the pod's air within 90 seconds, drawing out all residual scent molecules before the next person enters. This is separate from the regular HVAC airflow.

Without this, a person booking the Shimla pine forest would open the door to leftover temple incense from the previous guest. The exhaust cycle ensures every session begins on a completely clean sensory slate — as if the pod had never been used.

Session Booking

Stay longer
in off-peak hours

The standard session is 15 minutes. But during non-peak hours, users can double or triple book consecutive slots for a 30- or 45-minute experience — at the same per-session rate.

Peak and non-peak windows are set per location by the venue partner. The app displays real-time availability and automatically enables multi-booking when consecutive slots are open.
